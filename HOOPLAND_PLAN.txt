HOOP LAND ACCESS - THE PLAN
Written 15 November 2026

WHAT IS GOOD NEWS
- The game's code has readable names for the important things: the 10 players,
  who has the ball, who you are, your pass target, "in position" for a shot,
  "is open", the shot meter, and even the game's own play-by-play text.
- The game has Offensive and Defensive Assistance, but (you told me) only
  after you have a created or solo player. So the mod gives its own spoken
  help in the modes where those are locked.

THE ONE BIG RISK
- The game scrambles most of its inner names, and the scramble changes with
  every game update. The old mod used scrambled names in places, so updates
  break it. The new mod will only use the readable names, so updates should
  break much less. After each game update I still re-check it.

BUILD ORDER (each step tested by you before the next)
1. Base: the mod loads, speaks through JAWS, reads every menu item.
   Team and player screens read with ratings and stats.
2. Game info on keys: score, clock, quarter, shot clock, who has the ball.
   The game's own play-by-play spoken: "Smith steals the ball", "Jones,
   3 pointer, good". Short.
3. Shooting: one rising tone while you hold shoot, top = release.
   The game's shot result spoken: perfect, early, late, made, missed.
   Where you are: "left wing, behind the arc, 24 feet".
4. Passing: who is open, spoken by name and spot. Your pass target spoken
   when it changes.
5. Defense: "Defense, you guard Smith, he is left of you". Game's defensive
   assistance on. One cue when your man drives past you.
6. Career and season: schedule, stats, contracts, trades, upgrades.
7. Extras: 3-point contest, free throws, timeouts, subs, calling plays.

WHAT IS NOT REASONABLE OR EXTREMELY HARD
- Fully manual defense with no help: staying in front of a fast guard,
  timing blocks and steals. Extremely hard by ear. Plan: game's defensive
  assistance + a few spoken cues. You still choose when to steal or jump.
- Knowing where all 10 players are at once. Nobody can follow that by ear.
  We tell you only what matters right now: your spot, ball, open teammate,
  your man.
- Fast breaks and quick passes in traffic: possible, but you will be slower
  than a sighted player at first. Difficulty setting Easy helps.
- Dunk contest (button combos in mid-air): very hard. Maybe later, low priority.
- Court, jersey and logo designer: visual tools. We can read color names,
  but designing looks is not worth the effort.
- Replays and GIF sharing: visual only. Skipped.
- Pixel-perfect things like exact player sprite looks: skipped.

VERY DOABLE
- All menus, career, franchise, stats, trades, drafts.
- Shooting with a timing tone. Free throws. 3-point contest.
- Offense: dribble to a spot, hear "good shot spot", shoot or pass.
- Simulating games and running a team as coach.
