HOOP LAND ACCESS - RECAP OF EVERYTHING SO FAR
Updated 15 November 2026, mod version 0.6.0

========================================
1. MY HONEST VERDICT
========================================
- Hoop Land can become a game you can really play: about 70 to 80 percent chance.
- Why it is hopeful:
  - The court is small and flat (2D, seen from the side). No rooms, stairs or walls.
  - Only 10 players and 1 ball. The mod can always know where everyone is.
  - Menus, career, franchise, stats, trades: close to 90 percent doable.
  - The shot meter can become a sound you time your release to.
  - The game's code has readable names for the important things: the players,
    who has the ball, your pass target, "in position" for a shot, "is open",
    the shot meter, and the game's own play-by-play text.
- What is hard:
  - Real games are fast. Too much talking and you fall behind, too little and
    you are lost. Getting the balance right needs testing with you.
  - Defense is the hardest part.
  - Quick passes need a fast, smart way to pick a teammate.

4. WHAT IS NOT REASONABLE OR EXTREMELY HARD
========================================
- Fully manual defense with no help: staying in front of a fast guard, timing
  blocks and steals by ear. Plan: spoken help, you still choose when to steal
  or jump.
- You told me the game's Offensive and Defensive Assistance only unlock with a
  created or solo player. So the mod gives its own help where they are locked.
- Knowing where all 10 players are at once. Nobody can follow that by ear.
  The mod tells you only what matters now: your spot, the ball, the open
  teammate, your man.
- Fast breaks and quick passes in traffic: possible, but slower than a sighted
  player at first. An easier difficulty helps.
- Dunk contest (button combos in mid-air): very hard. Maybe later.
- Court, jersey and logo designer: visual tools. Skipped (color names can be read).
- Replays and GIF sharing: visual only. Skipped.

VERY DOABLE
- All menus, career, franchise, stats, trades, drafts.
- Shooting with a timing tone. Free throws. 3-point contest.
- Offense: move to a spot, hear "good shot spot", shoot or pass.
- Simulating games and running a team as coach.

7. KEYS (MENUS)
========================================
Keyboard (the game itself has no keyboard menu control, the mod adds it):
- Up / Down arrow: previous / next item (Tab and Shift+Tab also work)
- Left / Right arrow: change choice, or move inside a row
- Enter or Space: press / pick
- Escape or Backspace: Back (presses the screen's Back or Cancel button)
- Home / End: first / last item
- F1: repeat current item, with its place ("3 of 11")
- F2: read everything on the screen
- F11: straight-list menus on/off (saved)
- F12: save this screen to a file for Claude (when something reads wrong)
Controller:
- D-pad: move (straight list), A pick, B back (game's own)
- Right stick click: repeat current item
- Left stick click: read the whole screen
During a match (F-keys never clash with the game's own play keys):
- F5: score, quarter, clock, shot clock
- F6: who has the ball
- F7: shot tone on/off (saved)
- F8: rim distance and direction
- Escape: pause (the game's own key; the mod helps if it does not pause)
Controller during a match (I read the game's own button list; these are the
only buttons the game does not use in play):
- RT: score, quarter, clock, shot clock (same as F5)
- LT: who has the ball (same as F6)
- R3 (click right stick): rim distance (same as F8)
- L3 (click left stick): repeat the last commentator line
- F7 (keyboard): shot help: rising tone, count-in beeps, or off

HOW TO SHOOT
- Xbox: HOLD B. Keyboard: HOLD Right arrow. The meter starts when you press.
  Let go to shoot. Holding too short = TOO EARLY, too long = TOO LATE.
- Rising tone mode: let go when the tone stops climbing (its highest pitch).
- Count-in mode: you hear beep, beep, BEEP. Let go on the third, higher beep.
  The mod predicts the green zone from the meter's speed (tested: within about
  20 thousandths of a second).

THE GAME'S BUTTONS (from its own settings)
- Move: left stick or D-pad. Right stick: special moves.
- Offense with ball: A pass, B shoot, X cross, Y step, LB call play, RB icon pass.
- Offense off ball: A call for ball, B set screen, X teammate shoot, Y rebound, RB icon switch.
- Defense: A switch player, B take charge, X steal, Y block, LB lock defense, RB icon switch.
- View: timeout. Menu: pause. LB: quick subs.

8. THE GAME'S OWN KEYBOARD CONTROLS FOR PLAYING (found in the pause screen)
========================================
Yes, the game can be played on keyboard. The mod will use only F-keys during
matches so it never blocks these.
- Move: W A S D
- Offense with the ball: Shoot = hold Right arrow. Pass = Down arrow.
  Lob pass = hold Down. Pump fake = Right. Step = Up. Switch hands = Left.
  Hesitation = hold Left. Icon pass = Right Shift. Call play = Shift.
- Off the ball: Call for ball = Down. Call for lob = hold Down.
  Set screen = hold Right. Teammate shoot = Left. Rebound = Up.
- Defense: Steal = Left. Block / rebound = Up. Take charge = hold Right.
  Switch player = Down. Intentional foul = hold Left. Lock defense = hold Shift.
  Icon switch = Right Shift.
- Call timeout: Space.
(I read these from the screen; some rows may be mixed up between offense and
defense. We will confirm them in play.)

========================================
Built and checked by Claude in 7 test matches (Boston vs Atlanta exhibition,
Player 1 on keyboard). Installed. Full key list: Downloads\HoopLand_Keys.txt.

DEFENSE
- "Defense. You guard Young." The mod uses the game's own matchup (your
  position against his), so it stays steady.
- "Past you, left!" when your man gets nearer your hoop than you.
- "Steal!" when your man has the ball and is in reach.
- "Jump!" when an opponent near you starts a shot.
- F4 (or right stick click on defense): "Guarding Young, he has the ball,
  9 feet, right. Step right and down." or "Good spot, you are between him
  and the hoop."

LINES
- "Sideline!" / "Baseline!" about a second before you step out, only while
  you have the ball (checked: warning came 0.8 s before the whistle).
- "Half court! Don't go back." with the ball near the half-court line.
- "Bring the ball over half court, go right." after 3 seconds in your back
  court (the game calls an 8-second violation).

PASSING
- "Pass: Hauser, covered, right." your pass target while you hold the ball,
  and "Hauser open." when he gets open.
- F3 (or left stick click on offense): all teammates, nearest first, open

  it also says each teammate's icon key, for example "icon Left arrow".
- "Hauser inbounds. Call for the ball: A. Keyboard: Down arrow." This was
  the cause of all the five-second violations: when a teammate inbounds,
  the game waits for you to call for the ball. Checked: pressing Down works.
